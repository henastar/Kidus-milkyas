Stories of Transformation

Witness the power of collective compassion. Every donation tells a story.

Shelter & Reintegration

A Second Chance for Abebe

After losing his livelihood, Abebe received rental assistance and job skills training. Today, he runs a small carpentry workshop and supports his family.

Impact: 100% Self-reliance, Rent Covered, 3 children in school.
Education Support

Bright Future for Hana

Hana was unable to enroll in school due to costs. Through Mercy Bridge sponsor kits, she received textbooks, uniform, and tuition. She is now top of her class.

Impact: Educational costs fully covered, Academic Excellence.
Community Mobilization

Rebuilding After Fire

A local community center was damaged by fire. 5 local churches and 80 volunteers gathered through our platform to fully rebuild it in under three weeks.

Impact: Community center reconstructed, 5 churches united.
