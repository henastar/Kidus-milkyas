About Mercy Bridge Platform

Learn about our origin, mission, values, and the team driving our platform.

Our Mission

To provide a transparent digital platform that connects the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church with donors, volunteers, and beneficiaries; raises public awareness of church social services; mobilizes resources; and facilitates efficient, equitable, and sustainable service delivery for the benefit of communities.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital platform that strengthens the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church's social service by connecting churches, donors, volunteers, and beneficiaries, while promoting faith, compassion, transparency, and strengthening a sustainable social service system.

Core Values

Compassion

We serve with empathy, respect, and deep care for every human being.

Transparency

We ensure every donation and volunteer effort directly impacts the designated need.

Unity

We bridge differences to unite churches, organizations, and individuals in service.

Dignity

We uphold the dignity and privacy of those who seek help, promoting self-reliance.
