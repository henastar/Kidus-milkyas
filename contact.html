Frequently Asked Questions

How does Mercy Bridge ensure transparency?

Every financial donation is matched with a specific Need ID. Progress is tracked publicly on the needs page, and verified receipts/reports are uploaded by the partner church coordinator.

Can I request support for a relative in another town?

Yes. You can submit a request on their behalf. We will match them with the nearest partner church in our directory to verify their needs locally.

Is there any charge for platform services?

No. Mercy Bridge is 100% free for beneficiaries, partner churches, and volunteers. Operating costs are funded by specific donor grants.
